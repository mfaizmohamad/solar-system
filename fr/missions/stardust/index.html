Description publiée par l’archive

The Stardust Mission, launched in February 1999, was the f1rst to collect and return samples of cometary and interplanetary dust. The primary mission included a flyby of asteroid (5535) Annefrank in November 2002, on the way to the flyby of long-period Comet 81P/Wild 2 in January 2004. The samples collected (in aerogel) were successfully returned to Earth in January 2006. The spacecraft was redirected to Comet 9P/Tempel 1 in its extended mission (NExT - New Exploration of Tempel 1).
