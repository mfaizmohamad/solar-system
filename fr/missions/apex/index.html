Description publiée par l’archive

The OSIRIS-APEX mission is an extended mission of the OSIRiS-REx mission. APEX uses the capable OSIRIS-REX spacecraft with its complete instrument suite. APEX will distantly observe Apophis during the 2029 Earth encounter and capture its evolution in real time, revealing the consequences of an asteroid undergoing tidal disturbance by a major planet. Beginning in 2029 June, the spacecraft’s instrument suite will begin providing high resolution data of this “stony” asteroid — advancing knowledge of these objects and their connection to meteorites.
