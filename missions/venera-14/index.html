Description published by the archive

The Soviet Venera 14 mission and its twin Venera 13 formed a pair of identical spacecraft, each comprising of a flyby carrier bus and a descent craft (lander probe), built to take advantage of the 1981 Venus launch opportunity. Launched five days apart, the objective of the missions was to make in-situ studies of the Venus atmosphere and surface.
