Description published by the archive

As part of NASA’s CLPS (Commercial Lunar Payload Services) initiative and Artemis campaign, Firefly’s Blue Ghost lunar lander delivered ten NASA science and technology instruments to Mare Crisium on the near side of the Moon. The CLPS name for the delivery is CLPS Task Order (TO) 19D and the Firefly Aerospace mission name is Blue Ghost Mission 1. Blue Ghost Mission 1 (BGM1) launched on Jan 15, 2025 and landed on March 2, 2025. The surface mission extended through one lunar day and multiple hours into the lunar night before concluding on March 16, 2025.
