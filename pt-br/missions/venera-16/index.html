Descrição publicada pelo arquivo

The Soviet Venera 16 and Venera 15 missions consisted of two identical spacecraft that used 8 cm band side-looking radar to map the surface of Venus. The spacecraft entered nearly polar orbits a day apart with orbital planes shifted 4.45 deg, enabling reimaging capabilities. Both had periapsis at 62 deg N latitude and together mapped from the north pole to 30 deg N latitude with 1-2 km resolution over 8 months of operations.
